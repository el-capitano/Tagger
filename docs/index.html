Faces, objects and people counting.

Multiple appearances. One person.
Capture different expressions and looks without creating duplicate identities.
Identify common objects.
Label supported vehicles, animals, everyday objects and broader scenes.
Count everyone in view.
Include unregistered people. Use the counter independently of face recognition.
Choose your camera view.
Switch cameras, rotate to landscape, go fullscreen and customize overlays.

Profiles stay on your phone.

Camera frames

Processed in memory, without saving images. Bundled models run locally; the app has no Internet permission.

Registered profiles

Encrypted with AES-GCM and Android Keystore. Register people with permission, and delete their profiles whenever needed.

Get Tagger on Android.

Build the APK from source, then install it on your device.

Android 8.0 or newer. Follow the build guide to set up Android Studio.
